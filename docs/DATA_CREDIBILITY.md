# MetroPulse Data Credibility Guide

## How MetroPulse Works

MetroPulse is an **interactive urban scenario sandbox** that combines real-time signals, estimated city baselines, historical evidence retrieval, and transparent simulation models to explore potential consequences of economic and infrastructure interventions.

It is **not** a prediction engine, forecasting platform, or decision-making system.

---

## Data Sources — What's Real, Estimated, and Synthetic

### Real-Time Data (fetched from live APIs)

| Source | What It Does | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Google News RSS | City-specific economic news, article counts, keyword sentiment | 0.85 |
| GDELT API | News fallback with NLP-derived tone analysis | 0.85 |
| News Mobility Proxy | Transport/traffic news filtered for mobility sentiment | 0.70 |
| News Jobs Proxy | Employment/hiring/layoff news filtered for jobs sentiment | 0.70 |
| News Land Value Proxy | Real estate/rent/property news filtered for land value sentiment | 0.70 |
| Static Census | Population/demographics from city YAML config | 0.55 |

News sentiment is **real-time** but is a **proxy** — it tells you what people are talking about, not what is actually measured. A negative sentiment score means more negative news articles, not that GDP actually fell.

### Estimated Data (from city YAML baselines)

| Data | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Population | Census estimates (2011-2024) | 0.55 |
| GDP estimates | State GSDP apportionment | 0.55 |
| Employment figures | Estimated formal/informal splits | 0.55 |
| Sector weights | Estimated from economic structure | 0.55 |
| Unemployment rate | No city-level data exists in India | 0.55 |

These are **reasonable estimates** but not from official city-level surveys. India does not publish city-level GDP or unemployment data.

### Synthetic Data (generated by formulas)

| Data | How It's Generated | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cell-level economic activity | `exp(-distance_from_center * 2.8)` | 0.35 |
| Real estate index | `1.25 * exp(-distance * 1.8) + 0.35` | 0.35 |
| Congestion | `0.25 + 0.55 * exp(-distance * 1.4)` | 0.35 |
| Flood risk | Distance-to-water + port city heuristic | 0.35 |
| Migration pressure | Linear formula from housing + employment | 0.35 |

These are **spatial distribution models**, not measurements. They produce plausible patterns but are not derived from actual spatial data.

## Confidence Scoring

Every simulation output carries a confidence score (0-1):

| Range | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0.70 - 1.00 | High | Based on measured data |
| 0.40 - 0.69 | Medium | Based on estimated baselines |
| 0.00 - 0.39 | Low | Model-generated / synthetic |

Confidence **decays** each simulation month:
- Primary loop: ×0.90 per month (scenario shocks are well-understood)
- Secondary cascade: ×0.80 per month (spatial spillovers are less certain)
- Monsoon effects: ×0.75 (weather is hard to predict)

A cell that starts at 0.55 confidence will drop to ~0.35 after 6 months of simulation.

## How Real-Time Data Influences Simulation

When news connectors succeed, city-level signals are converted into per-cell modifiers:

1. **News sentiment** → economic modifier (0.85-1.15 multiplier on GDP)
2. **Jobs sentiment** → migration modifier (0.90-1.10)
3. **Average sentiment** → investment confidence (0.0-1.0)

These modifiers are applied during grid initialization and affect the simulation starting state. They do **not** change during the simulation loop.

If no real-time data is available, the simulation runs on pure city YAML baselines with synthetic spatial distribution.

## Provenance Tracking

Every cell and every aggregate metric carries provenance metadata:

- **origin**: `real` / `real_time_context` / `rag_evidence` / `estimated` / `synthetic` / `fallback`
- **confidence**: 0-1 score
- **sources**: list of data sources used

The frontend displays this in:
- **DataFreshnessPanel ("Simulation Trust")**: mode label + confidence score + domain freshness chips
- **MetricPanel**: per-metric confidence_label badge on summary pills + confidence/origin on detailed charts
- **Cell tooltip**: per-cell confidence percentage
- **Evidence Simple tab**: "Why this result?" section with top drivers, contribution %, and confidence
- **Evidence Technical tab**: per-metric data origins + overall confidence + data source audit

## RAG Evidence Engine

The RAG engine retrieves relevant Indian urban case studies to support coefficient selection and provide evidence-backed explanations.

**What it does:**
- Scans 15 hardcoded Indian urban case studies (Bengaluru IT, Hyderabad pharma, Delhi metro, Mumbai mill land, etc.)
- Computes relevance scores: city match (0.25) + sector overlap (0.45) + policy overlap (0.20) + keyword match (0.10)
- Adjusts sector coefficients within clamps [0.65, 1.10] based on evidence strength
- Provides confidence-weighted explanations

**What it does NOT do:**
- Replace simulation math with LLM-generated numbers
- Invent predictions without evidence support
- Claim causal certainty from historical parallels

## What MetroPulse IS

- A **scenario exploration tool** for understanding directional impacts
- A **what-if sandbox** for testing economic hypotheses
- A **visualization platform** for spatial economic patterns
- A **learning tool** for urban economics concepts

## What MetroPulse IS NOT

- A **prediction engine** that forecasts real-world outcomes
- A **decision support system** for policy implementation
- A **real-time digital twin** that mirrors actual city state
- A **validated model** with published accuracy metrics
